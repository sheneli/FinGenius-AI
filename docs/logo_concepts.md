# Logo Concepts — FinGenius AI

Three original directions for an abstract **FG monogram** expressing upward momentum, intelligence (connected nodes), stability, and forward movement. No dollar signs, brains, piggy banks, coin stacks, clip-art charts, robot heads, or circuitry.

## Direction A — "Ascent Node"
An abstract F whose three horizontal strokes step upward like a rising staircase, terminating in a single luminous node; the G is implied by a partial orbit arc sweeping around the composition. Green→cyan gradient on deep navy.

## Direction C — "Orbit G" (selected)
A bold geometric G formed by an open ring with a flat inner shelf; inside it, an upward diagonal stroke with a stepped notch forms a minimal F that doubles as a rising path, ending in one accent node at the top-right — decision-point + momentum. Negative space does the work; the silhouette is a coin-like ring broken by ascent (stability + growth).

## Decision
**Direction C — Orbit G.** Highest total; strongest at launcher size, best monochrome behaviour, distinctive silhouette.

Construction: 512-unit grid; ring outer r=200, stroke 64; ring opening 55° at upper right; ascent stroke 64 wide at 45° with one 32-unit step notch (the F reference); accent node r=36 in Accent Gold placed in the ring gap. Gradient: `#10B981 → #22D3EE` at 45°. Dark surfaces use the gradient mark; light surfaces use navy `#071A2E` mark; monochrome uses a single colour.

Clear space: ½ ring diameter on all sides. Minimum sizes: mark 16 dp (mono), 24 dp (colour); lockups 96 dp wide. Prohibited: rotation, drop shadows, re-colouring outside approved pairs, placing the gradient mark on mid-grey backgrounds, adding text inside the app-icon version.
